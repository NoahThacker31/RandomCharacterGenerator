# Quest dropdown sources

Quest titles and progression were checked against these quest/faction guides:

- [Main Quest and Mages Guild quests](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Quests_(Oblivion))
- [Fighters Guild](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Fighters_Guild_(Oblivion))
- [Thieves Guild](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Thieves_Guild_(Oblivion))
- [Dark Brotherhood](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Brotherhood_(Oblivion))
- [Knights of the Nine](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Quests_(Knights_of_the_Nine))
- [Shivering Isles](https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/Quests_(Shivering_Isles))

The dropdown means **last quest completed**. Selecting the final listed quest marks the line complete. Main Quest includes the final Imperial Dragon Armor reward quest. Dark Brotherhood ends at Honor Thy Mother; Whispers of Death is repeatable. Guild expulsions, ongoing tracking quests, and optional post-story tasks are excluded. Recommendation quests and some guild jobs can be completed in different orders; the list provides one valid progression order. Shivering Isles lists both mutually exclusive Ritual choices at the same stage; players can skip the other branch.

Legacy text milestones remain available as a labeled legacy dropdown option. Legacy completed flags are converted to the final story quest. The original JSON schema stays compatible.

Parallel stages use shared display numbers, including all seven recommendations (stage 2), followed by A Mage's Staff (stage 3). Artifact quests can be completed in either order: https://www.gamespot.com/articles/the-elder-scrolls-iv-oblivion-walkthrough/1100-6147085/ . Shivering Isles Addiction and The Lady of Paranoia are unlocked together: https://game8.co/games/Elder-Scrolls-Oblivion/archives/522188 . New and previously empty Main Quest milestones start at Tutorial.
